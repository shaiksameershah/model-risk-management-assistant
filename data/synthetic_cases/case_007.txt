The Legacy Fraud Scoring Model (LFS-2018-003) is a logistic regression model
that was historically used by the Fraud Operations function to score card-not-
present transactions. The model is being sunset; the replacement model
(NFS-2025-001) went live in January 2026, and LFS-2018-003 is scheduled for
decommissioning at the end of Q1 2026.

Inputs were historical transaction, merchant, and device attributes sourced from
the payments data warehouse. Outputs were a fraud probability score (0-1) used to
route transactions for manual review.

The model was last validated on 2022-11-10. It is in a decommissioning state and
no longer used for live decisions. Monitoring has been reduced to periodic review
of residual traffic. Known weakness: degraded performance on newer payment types
introduced after 2023; no remediation is planned because the model is being retired.
