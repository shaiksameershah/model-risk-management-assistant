The Customer Service Summarisation Model (CSS-2025-002) is an LLM-based system
built on an open-source foundation model fine-tuned on internal call transcripts.
It is used by the Contact Centre Operations function to generate short summaries
of customer calls for agent review.

Inputs are call transcripts and associated metadata (call duration, product line,
customer segment). Outputs are 3-5 sentence summaries and a small set of
topic tags. Downstream uses include quality assurance sampling and compliance
review.

The model is in production in a limited pilot. Validation is currently in progress;
the first formal validation has not yet been completed. Known concerns include
hallucination risk on low-quality transcripts and potential bias in topic tagging
across customer segments, both under evaluation by the second line.
