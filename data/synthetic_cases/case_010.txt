The Market Risk VaR Model (MRV-2021-020) is used by the Markets Risk function to
compute 1-day 99% Value-at-Risk for the trading book. It is a historical
simulation model implemented in C++ with Python wrappers.

Inputs are position data from the trading systems and 500 days of historical
market data. Outputs are VaR and Expected Shortfall estimates at portfolio and
desk level. Downstream uses include limit monitoring and regulatory capital
calculations.

The model was last validated on 2023-04-12 according to the validation tracker.
However, the annual model review document lists the most recent validation as
2024-09-30.

The model is in production. Monitoring includes daily backtesting and monthly
independent replication.
