The Trade Surveillance Alert Scoring Model (TSA-2021-005) is used by the Markets
Compliance function to rank post-trade surveillance alerts for analyst review.
It is a vendor-supplied system from Acme Surveillance Ltd; the bank has no source
code access and relies on the vendor's model documentation.

Inputs are trade and order records from the equities and fixed-income trading
platforms, plus counterparty reference data from the bank's golden source. Outputs
are alert risk scores on a 0-100 scale. Downstream uses include prioritisation of
the analyst review queue.

The model is in production. It was last reviewed by the bank in 2023-08. The vendor
performs its own validation, and the bank has a periodic attestation process.
Monitoring includes alert volume, precision, and coverage reviews.
