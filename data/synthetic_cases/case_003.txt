The AML Transaction Monitoring Model (AML-2022-008) is a rules-based and
gradient-boosted hybrid model operated by the Financial Crime Compliance function.
It generates alerts for potentially suspicious transactions, which are reviewed by
analysts. The development team is the Financial Crime Analytics group.

Inputs are retail and commercial transaction records from the core banking platform
and a curated sanctions and PEP list. Outputs are per-customer suspicion scores and
alert flags. Downstream uses include case generation for the investigations team.

Given the high sensitivity of the outputs, the model is subject to enhanced oversight.
The model is currently in production. It was last validated on 2023-11-02 and is
monitored through monthly alert-rate and precision reviews.

Known weakness: elevated false-positive rate in the small-business segment, currently
under remediation. The team has documented a plan to retune thresholds in the next
quarter.
