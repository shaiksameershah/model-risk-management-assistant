The ESG Portfolio Screening Model (ESG-2024-031) is used by the Sustainable
Finance function to screen corporate lending exposures against the bank's
sustainability policy framework. It applies a rules-based scoring methodology
combining sector classifications, third-party ESG ratings, and internal policy
overrides.

Inputs are exposure-level corporate loan data, third-party ESG ratings from two
providers, and the policy framework reference tables. Outputs are ESG classification
labels (Red, Amber, Green) per exposure. Downstream uses include portfolio
reporting, regulatory disclosures, and client engagement prioritisation.

The model was deployed to production in 2024-06. It has never been validated and
no validation is currently planned. Monitoring is limited to quarterly rule-change
review. Known limitation: dependence on third-party ESG ratings introduces provider
methodology risk. The provider dependency has been flagged for review.
