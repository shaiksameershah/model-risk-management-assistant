The Retail Credit Scorecard (RC-2024-017) is a logistic regression model used by the
Consumer Lending business function to estimate probability of default for unsecured
personal loans at origination. The business owner is Priya Raman, Head of Consumer Credit.
The model owner is Daniel Okafor, Senior Manager, Credit Analytics. The development team
is the Credit Modelling Group.

The model is developed in Python 3.11 and deployed on the internal scoring platform.
Inputs are bureau attributes from Experian and internal application data. Outputs are a
PD estimate on a 0-1 scale and a risk band. Downstream uses include pricing and limit
assignment. The model was last validated on 2024-03-15.

Key assumption: the relationship between bureau attributes and default is stable over a
12-month horizon. Limitation: the model does not cover secured lending.

The model is currently in production. Monitoring includes monthly PSI and quarterly
discrimination review.

We have not yet documented the third-party dependencies or the remediation actions
from the last validation.
