The Wholesale Credit Stress Model (WCS-2023-041) is used by the Corporate Banking
business function to project credit losses under adverse scenarios. The model owner
is Anna Lindqvist, Director, Credit Risk Analytics. Separately, ownership is listed
as the "Wholesale Modelling Team" in the development plan.

The model is a Monte Carlo simulation implemented in Python. Inputs are the internal
loan book, macroeconomic scenarios from the Treasury scenario library, and historical
default and recovery rates. Outputs are projected credit losses at the portfolio level
and by industry segment. Downstream uses include capital planning and stress-testing
submissions.

The model was last validated in Q3 2024. It is currently in production. Monitoring
includes quarterly backtesting of projected versus realised losses.

No third-party model dependencies are used. Key limitation: coverage is limited to the
corporate loan portfolio and excludes traded credit products.
