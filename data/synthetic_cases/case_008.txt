The Interest Rate Risk Model (IRR-2020-014) is used by the Treasury function to
measure the sensitivity of the bank's net interest income to parallel and
non-parallel yield curve shifts. It is a deterministic scenario-based model
implemented in MATLAB.

Inputs are the current balance sheet position, contractual cash flow schedules,
and yield curve scenarios from the risk systems. Outputs are projected NII at
multiple time horizons and across defined scenarios. Downstream uses include
ALCO reporting and IRRBB regulatory submissions.

The model was last validated on 15 March 2024. It is in production. Monitoring
includes monthly sensitivity re-runs and quarterly independent replication.
Known assumption: constant prepayment rates across scenarios; this assumption is
documented in the model methodology note.
